Executive Summary

Understanding labor market trends is essential for workforce planning, economic forecasting, and strategic decision-making. This project analyzes benchmark-revised employment data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) to uncover employment concentration, industry growth patterns, workforce stability, and seasonal hiring trends. The final deliverable was an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to transform raw labor market data into actionable business insights.

Business Problem

Large employment datasets can be difficult to interpret without proper analysis. The goal was to identify which industries employ the most workers, which sectors are growing or declining, how concentrated employment is across industries, and which industries display strong seasonal hiring patterns.

ETL Process

Extract

Retrieved benchmark-revised employment data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

Transform

Removed duplicates, standardized industry names, handled missing data, created date dimensions, calculated growth metrics, and generated rankings.

Load

Loaded transformed data into SQL Server and connected Tableau to create the final analytical dashboard.

Data Model

Implemented a dimensional star schema consisting of a fact_employment table connected to industry and date dimensions to support efficient reporting, trend analysis, and dashboard performance.

Key Findings

  • Health Care & Social Assistance represented approximately 21% of total employment.
  • Healthcare recorded the strongest year-over-year growth at 3.5%.
  • More than 41% of employment was concentrated within two industries.
  • Information experienced the largest decline at 2.1%.
  • Construction, Retail Trade, and Leisure & Hospitality displayed the strongest seasonality.

Recommendations

  • Monitor Healthcare as a leading indicator of labor market growth.
  • Account for seasonality when evaluating workforce trends.
  • Investigate declining industries for long-term labor market shifts.
  • Expand future analysis with unemployment, inflation, and GDP indicators.

Business Value

This project demonstrates how raw labor market data can be transformed into an interactive analytical solution that supports workforce planning, economic analysis, and strategic decision-making through business intelligence, ETL development, dimensional modeling, advanced SQL analysis, and dashboarding.
