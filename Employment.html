Executive Summary

Understanding labor market trends is essential for workforce planning, economic forecasting, and strategic decision making. In this project, I analyzed benchmark-revised employment data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics BLS) to uncover employment concentration, industry growth patterns, workforce stability, and seasonal hiring trends across major sectors of the U.S. economy.


To accomplish this, I developed a complete end-to-end analytics solution that included data extraction, transformation, database design, advanced SQL analysis, dimensional modeling, and interactive dashboard development. The final result is a Tableau dashboard that transforms complex labor market data into actionable business insights.

Key Results

  • Health Care & Social Assistance represented approximately 21.0% of total U.S. employment.

  • Health Care & Social Assistance recorded the strongest year-over-year growth at 3.5%.

  • More than 41% of total U.S. employment was concentrated within two industries.

  • Information experienced the largest employment decline at 2.1%.

  • Construction, Retail Trade, and Leisure & Hospitality exhibited the strongest seasonal hiring patterns.

  • Employment growth was concentrated within a limited number of industries rather than being broadly distributed across the labor market.

Business Problem

Organizations, economists, and workforce planners rely on labor market data to understand economic conditions, workforce demand, and industry performance. However, large employment datasets can be difficult to interpret without proper analysis.


The goal of this project was to transform raw employment data into an analytical solution capable of answering key business questions:


  • Which industries employ the most workers?

  • Which industries are experiencing growth or decline?

  • How concentrated is employment across major sectors?

  • Which industries demonstrate the most stable employment trends?

  • Which industries exhibit significant seasonal hiring fluctuations?

By answering these questions, decision-makers can better understand workforce dynamics and identify meaningful labor market trends.
