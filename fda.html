FDA Recall Risk & Severity Analysis (2024-2025)

SQL • Tableau • Excel • Risk Analytics

Dashboard Preview

Executive Summary

  • This project analyzes U.S. FDA recall data from 2024–2025 to identify trends in recall frequency, classification severity, and affected product categories.

  • The analysis reveals that 2025 did not experience more recalls — it experienced more severe recalls, with food products accounting for the highest proportion of high-risk recalls.

  • These findings help manufacturers and regulators identify high-risk segments and improve preventive monitoring strategies.

Business Problem

How did recall risk and severity change from 2024 to 2025, and where should regulators focus preventive efforts?

Key Findings

  • Total recalls increased slightly (2.4%).

  • Class I recalls increased 15.4% year-over-year.

  • Food contamination was the dominant driver.

  • California showed the highest year-over-year increase.

Business Impact

2025 saw higher public health risk despite stable overall recall volume.

Business Context & Objectives

Why It Matters


  • Product recalls can result in financial loss, brand damage, and public health risk.

  • This analysis aims to identify patterns in recall severity and frequency to support proactive risk management and regulatory monitoring.

Target Stakeholders


  • Primary: FDA Regulatory & Compliance Officers

  • Secondary: Food Manufacturers & Quality Assurance Teams

Project Objectives

  • Understand recall frequency trends between 2024 and 2025.

  • Determine whether the most common recall classification changed year-over-year.

  • Evaluate differences in monthly recall trends between 2024 and 2025.

  • Identify which states experienced the largest increases or decreases in recalls.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Recall Volume

Definition: Total number of FDA recall events per year.


  • 2024: 1,539 recalls

  • 2025: 1,576 recalls

  • Year-over-Year Change: +37 recalls (+2.4%)

Why it matters:

Measures overall recall activity and regulatory workload. While volume remained similar, small increases may signal early shifts in compliance trends.



High-Risk Recall Rate (Class I %)

Definition: Percentage of total recalls classified as Class I (highest severity).


  • 2024: 43% (667 of 1,539 recalls)

  • 2025: 49% (770 of 1,576 recalls)

  • Change: +6 percentage points

  • Class I volume increase: +103 recalls (+15.4%)

Why it matters:

Indicates whether recalls are becoming more serious. Although total recalls only rose 2.4%, high-risk recalls increased significantly, showing a shift toward greater public health exposure.



Recall Closure Rate

Definition: Percentage of recalls that were terminated (officially resolved).


  • 2024: 88% terminated (1,358 of 1,539)

  • 2025: 45% terminated (703 of 1,576)

  • Ongoing in 2025: 49% (778 recalls)

  • Change: −43 percentage points in termination rate

Why it matters:

Measures operational efficiency and resolution speed. The sharp drop in closure rate suggests a growing backlog or slower recall resolution in 2025.



Seasonal Spike Index

Definition: Measures how much the highest recall month exceeds the average monthly baseline.


  • 2025 Monthly Average: 131 recalls

  • Peak Month (June 2025): 303 recalls

  • Spike Index: 2.3× above average

Why it matters:

Identifies seasonal surges in recall activity. Significant spikes may require temporary increases in monitoring and inspection resources.

Dataset & Methodology

Dataset Source: FDA Recall Dashboard

Time Period: 2024–2025

Datatype: Public Government Data

Row Count: 3,116

Data Preparation & Cleaning

  • Standardized inconsistent text fields (e.g., recall reasons and state names) to prevent fragmented groupings in aggregate queries.

  • Converted recall date fields into proper date format to enable accurate year-over-year and monthly trend analysis.

  • Created derived fields (recall year, recall month) to support time-based aggregations.

  • Removed non-essential metadata columns to streamline query performance and focus analysis on severity, geography, and recall cause.

  • Reviewed null or missing state values to ensure accurate state-level comparisons.

FDA Recall Class Definitions

  • Class I – High risk; reasonable probability of serious health consequences or death.

  • Class II – Temporary or medically reversible health effects.

  • Class III – Unlikely to cause adverse health consequences.


Execute Query 
-- This query breaks down FDA recalls by recall class
-- for the years 2024 and 2025

SELECT

    YEAR(recall_date) AS recall_year,
    recall_class,
    COUNT(*) AS recall_count

FROM fda_recalls

WHERE YEAR(recall_date) IN (2024, 2025)

GROUP BY
    YEAR(recall_date),
    recall_class

ORDER BY
    recall_year,
    recall_count DESC;
  • Total recalls remained consistent year-over-year (+2.4%, from 1,539 to 1,576), but severity increased in 2025.

  • Class I recalls rose significantly from 667 to 770, increasing their share from 43% to 49%. Meanwhile, Class II recalls declined from 805 to 718.

Takeaway

2025 did not see more recalls overall — it saw more high-risk recalls.
